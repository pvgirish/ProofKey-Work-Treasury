## Review decisions

The new reviewer retracts the surface-count penalty and confirms the three app gaps. Adopt fresh-versus-cached labels, fixed-destination visibility, fresh imported-report reconstruction, and a short navigable payment story. Do not inherit revised numerical rankings or predictions about individual judges. A bounded primary-source check did not verify the claim about AMA Q4/writability; no new reverse-messaging requirement is needed to justify the selected scope.

The competitive improvement is operability and inspectability: a new reviewer can trace a payment, a replacement operator can prepare exact allowed calls, and another browser can reconstruct a report. This supports the named Attestcoin-depth and working-product criteria without adding artificial protocol surfaces. It is not evidence for a guaranteed rank or a numerical score increase.

A public program closeout is allocation/epoch accounting. Summed epoch caps may include reused funds; do not label those sums as unique deposits or claim a global 143-in/143-out audit unless global deposit/withdrawal records are independently checked.
