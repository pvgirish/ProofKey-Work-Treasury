# Technical-work program pilot

Status: recruitment and observation template. No organization or provider has been enrolled by this document. The latest [participant product guide](PARTICIPANT-PRODUCT.md) connects buyer offers, saved worker jobs, repeat work and payment records; [its progress](PARTICIPANT-PRODUCT-PROGRESS.md) records acceptance. Earlier browser inspection/settlement additions are recorded in [app workflow progress](APP-WORKFLOW-PROGRESS.md). Use the corresponding verified build for a pilot.

## The proposed workflow

A protocol team or ecosystem program keeps vendor approvals in its existing Ethereum Safe and evaluates one bounded CTC budget for planned technical review, remediation and retest. Workers review the full terms and funding evidence before signing. Attestcoin carries the source-final outcome into Creditcoin payment recognition. A replacement operator can continue supported settlement without changing the committed payee, and the program produces a verifiable accounting closeout.

This is a pilot of work authorization and payment operations. It does not certify software security or delivered-work quality, provide urgent incident-response coverage, or create a lending product.

## Qualify the premise before adding scope

Ask the prospective buyer to describe:

1. Which Safe currently authorizes vendors, its actual chain, and why those approvals should remain there.
2. Why a CTC budget is useful to the organization, including how price exposure and future production funding would be handled.
3. Its current direct-payment or per-job-escrow process, where repeated approvals or reconciliation cause friction, and which planned work is suitable for an evaluation.

A willingness to sign a test transaction is not evidence that this premise holds. Record objections and rejected alternatives. If the buyer does not need both its Ethereum authority and a CTC budget, reconsider this market hypothesis.

The current source is Sepolia and target is Creditcoin testnet. Using an actual mainnet Safe requires a separately verified domain-matched source/target deployment; a test Safe does not preserve mainnet governance. A mainnet source with test-CTC target payments remains a workflow evaluation, not production revenue.

## Participant control and consent

Use the [independent-settlement record](INDEPENDENT-SETTLEMENT.md). Record actual relationships, who controls each key, who supplies fees/test funds or separate compensation, assistance provided, and permission to publish selected facts. Never collect seed phrases or participant private keys.

Each worker reviews the exact scope, acceptance/revision terms, work and fee caps, beneficiary addresses, committee and block cutoffs. The funding observation is committed through the signed terms and checked against Creditcoin. It is not funding verification enforced by Ethereum. Ordinary source execution remains authoritative, and state can change after a review.

Public names, contact information and quotes require separate permission. The default evidence can use participant aliases. Do not name the host, a partner or a related company as a customer without their actual involvement and permission.

## What the buyer target means

A buyer is the organization commissioning and paying for work through ProofKey; it is not a person buying the CTC token or investing in the project. A repeat means that organization voluntarily chooses a later useful job. Named means it permits identification, and public closeout means it permits publication of the agreed transaction/evidence record. A testnet evaluation must remain labeled as a testnet evaluation. These are product-validation targets, not a newly asserted competition eligibility rule.

The software and record template can be implemented. Willing participants, their own decisions and their publication consent must come from those participants.

## Run and measure

- Begin with one useful scoped review and a separately agreed follow-up. Use the actual commercial outcome; do not create a cancellation, reduced payment or dispute for presentation value.
- Have worker and buyer use separate profiles/devices for authorization handoff. Record any step that requires team assistance, manual calldata or custody.
- Have the buyer prepare an unsigned offer in **Invite worker**, and the worker open it in **My work** before any wallet request. After signing and buyer acceptance, reopen the saved job and milestone on the worker's own device. Record whether the next-actor guidance is sufficient without team explanation.
- Record source approval, native authentication, recognition and successful payment receipts. Do not count an entitlement or failed transfer as payment.
- If unearned capacity naturally returns to available source capacity, record its use by a later job without a new funding transaction. RETURN itself permanently retires old-epoch capacity; demonstrate artificial branches only in separately labeled fixtures.
- After source finality, test whether a separately controlled operator can continue settlement from the exported packet with the project service unavailable. Distinguish cached authentication from a newly generated/native-verified witness. A total proof outage or rejecting recipient can require waiting or owner action.
- In the local upgraded app, a replacement operator can use **Payments → Continue verified payments** with the actual claim files. A reviewer can use **Check payment** to inspect the complete path.
- Have a fresh verifier use **Program → Verify program**, export the report and reimport it in a separate browser. Follow the [app workflow guide](APP-WORKFLOWS.md) to reconstruct the closeout. A WORK/FEE withdrawal is allocation-specific; a RETURN becomes owner-level fungible free balance, so later free withdrawals must not automatically be attributed to one epoch.
- Ask whether the buyer chooses to initiate another useful job. A repeat requested solely by the team is different from voluntary operational preference.
- If the buyer chooses a follow-up, use **Program → Create follow-up work** and obtain a fresh authorization. Record why this job was useful, whether released capacity was actually available and which steps still needed assistance.
- Rebuild a selected **Paid work** record, export it, and reconstruct the selection in another profile. Supply signed work files separately for a source-worker record. Preserve incomplete evidence rather than describing an RPC failure or entitlement as a verified payment. Time the reconciliation and note any archive-provider dependency.

Compare action count, assistance, approval-to-payment elapsed time, proof/finality waiting, fees and reconciliation effort with the buyer's current process. Record actual measurements and limitations; do not promise a percentage saving in advance.
